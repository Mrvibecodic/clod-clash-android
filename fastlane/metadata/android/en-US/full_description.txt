Clod Clash is an Android client for a subscription link issued by your provider.

<h3>Features</h3>
<ul>
<li>One-tap connection: paste the subscription link, the rest is pulled from it</li>
<li>Servers, groups and latency checks with favourites and search</li>
<li>Subscription status: traffic left, expiry date and reminders</li>
<li>Per-app routing: send every app through the tunnel or pick them one by one</li>
<li>Automatic subscription updates on a schedule</li>
<li>Quick settings tile, home screen widget and launcher shortcuts</li>
</ul>
