Clod Clash is an Android client that connects you to a VPN service from a subscription link.
The [Clod Core](https://github.com/Mrvibecodic/clod-core) core, our fork of the stable
[mihomo](https://github.com/MetaCubeX/mihomo), is built into the app and is updated together with it.

<Shot src="en/home.png" alt="Home screen: subscription, connect button, traffic and expiry left" />

## How it works

Your provider gives you a **subscription link**. The app downloads servers and rules from it,
and when you connect it brings up a tunnel that carries the phone's traffic. Traffic left,
expiry, announcements and support links come with the subscription.

## What it can do

- A device-wide tunnel or a local proxy without VPN.
- Manual server choice or automatic choice by latency, favourites.
- Apps that bypass the tunnel (or only they go through it).
- Several subscriptions, reminders before expiry and when traffic runs low.
- Dark theme, English and Russian, a widget, a quick settings tile, shortcuts.
- Built-in app updates, the core included.
