Clod Clash is an Android app that connects your phone to a VPN service using a subscription link.
The [Clod Core](https://github.com/Mrvibecodic/clod-core) core — our fork of the stable
[mihomo](https://github.com/MetaCubeX/mihomo) — is built into the app and updated together with it.

The documentation has two parts — pick yours. Screenshots show the Russian interface; the English
one looks the same.
