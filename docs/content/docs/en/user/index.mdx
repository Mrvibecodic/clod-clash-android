Clod Clash connects your phone to a VPN service using a **subscription link**. Your VPN service
(provider) gives you the link — in your customer portal, a bot or an email. From it the app
downloads the servers, rules, remaining traffic and expiry date by itself; you type nothing in by
hand.

<Shot src="ru/home.png" alt="Home screen after adding a subscription" />

## Five steps

<Steps>
<Step>

**Install the app.** Download the APK from the [download page](/en/download) and install it —
details in [Install](/en/docs/user/install).

</Step>
<Step>

**Add the subscription.** **Subscriptions** tab → **Add subscription** → paste the link →
**Add subscription**. Or open a `clodclash://install-config?url=…` link from your provider's portal and
save the subscription with the button in the header. Details in [Adding a subscription](/en/docs/user/subscription).

</Step>
<Step>

**Pick the apps for the VPN.** **More → Apps**. Right after installation no app goes through the
tunnel until you tick it (unless your provider sent its own list). Tick the ones you need or pick
**Allow all apps** — see [Basic setup](/en/docs/user/setup#which-apps-go-through-the-vpn).

</Step>
<Step>

**Connect.** Tap the big button on the home screen and allow the VPN in the system dialog. The
button turns green and a session timer starts inside it.

</Step>
<Step>

**Make the connection stable.** After the first connection the app offers once to lift the
battery restriction and turn on always-on VPN — accept, otherwise Android may put the tunnel to
sleep in the background.

</Step>
</Steps>

## What it can do

- A tunnel for the whole device, or a local proxy without a VPN.
- Pick a server by hand or let auto selection pick by delay; favorites.
- Choose which apps go through the tunnel (or, the other way round, around it).
- Several subscriptions, reminders about the end of the term and traffic.
- Dark theme, Russian and English, a widget, a quick settings tile, shortcuts.
- Built-in app updates together with the core.

<Callout title="Subscription support">
Clod Clash is a free app. Questions about your plan, payment, traffic and how the servers work go
to your provider. About the app itself — see [Help and contacts](/en/docs/user/help).
</Callout>
