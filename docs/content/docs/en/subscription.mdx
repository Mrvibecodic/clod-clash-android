## Adding a subscription

Open the **Subscriptions** tab, tap **+**, paste the link and tap "Add subscription".
The app downloads the server list and makes the subscription active right away.
Only `https` links are accepted — an address like `https://example.com/sub/…`.

You can keep several subscriptions — exactly one is active, and its name is shown in the
header of the home screen.

## Refreshing

A subscription refreshes itself on the interval set by the provider (usually every few hours).
To refresh right now, use the refresh icon in the home screen header or on the subscription card.

If a refresh fails, the app shows a notification and retries later. Notifications about
successful and failed refreshes are toggled separately in **More → Appearance and behaviour**.

## Traffic and expiry

While the tunnel is off, the home screen shows two cards: how much of your traffic package is
used and how many days are left. The same numbers are repeated in short next to the name in
the header.

<Shot src="en/home.png" alt="Traffic and expiry cards on the home screen" />

<Callout title="Where the numbers come from">
Your provider's panel sends them along with the subscription. If the provider does not send
them, the cards are simply absent — that is not an app error.
</Callout>

## Moving to another device

**More → Appearance and behaviour → Export subscriptions** writes the list of links to a file.
On the new device pick **Import subscriptions** and choose that file — links that are already
known will not be added twice.
