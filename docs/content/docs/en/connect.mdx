## Connecting

Tap the big button on the home screen. The first time, Android asks for the VPN permission —
you have to grant it, otherwise the tunnel cannot start.

Once connected, the button turns green, with the session timer underneath and counters for
what was downloaded and uploaded during this session.

<Callout title="The prompt on your first connection">
Once, the app shows a reminder titled "Keep the connection alive": it links to the system
battery and always-on settings. It is neither an ad nor an error — without those permissions
Android puts the tunnel to sleep. The same items live in **More → Connection** afterwards.
</Callout>

## Choosing a server

The **Selected server** row on the home screen opens the **Servers** tab with the full list
from your subscription. Tapping a row selects that node; you can pick one before connecting
and the choice is remembered.

<Shot src="en/servers.png" alt="The Servers tab: nodes with their latency" />

- **Latency** is the number on the right. Green up to 200 ms, amber up to 400 ms, red beyond.
  A cross means the node did not answer.
- **The star** marks a node as a favourite — favourites float to the top of the list.
- **The bolt** in the header measures latency again for the whole group.

When there is more than one group (say, "All servers" and "Streaming"), a group selector
appears above the list.

<Callout title="A dash instead of milliseconds">
If your provider disabled latency display, you get a tick, a cross or a dash instead of
numbers — the node is reachable, did not answer, or has not been probed yet.
</Callout>
