The project has two places in Telegram, and they are for different things.

## 📣 Group — news and releases

**[t.me/+2lmP1yhxpCE3MDcy](https://t.me/+2lmP1yhxpCE3MDcy)**

Release announcements, what changed, known issues and warnings. Follow it so
you do not miss an update; there is no discussion there.

## 💬 Chat — help and discussion

**[t.me/+8BJQXYXYLqM4YWYy](https://t.me/+8BJQXYXYLqM4YWYy)**

Questions about the app, troubleshooting, ideas and feature requests. Before
writing, check the [FAQ](/en/docs/faq) — half of the cases are covered there.

To get a quick answer, include:

- the app version, your Android version and phone model;
- what exactly you did and what you got;
- whether the app showed an error text, and which one.

## What is not for us

- **A subscription that does not work, traffic that ran out, questions about
  plans and payments** go to your provider, not to us: the project has no
  access to panels, servers or your data.
- **A bug in the app or a code proposal** is better as a
  [GitHub issue](https://github.com/Mrvibecodic/clod-clash-android/issues):
  it will not sink in the feed there.
