## The connection drops while the phone sits in my pocket

That is Android putting background apps to sleep. Three things help, all in
**More → Connection**:

1. **Battery restrictions** — remove the restriction for the app.
2. **Always-on connection** — enable it in the system VPN settings.
3. **Keep the tunnel awake with the screen off** — in the Network section.

Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung and Oppo phones add their own autostart and "memory protection"
lists — the app has to be added there by hand, it cannot do that for you.

## The server list is empty

Usually this is the subscription, not the app. The app spells out the reason:

- **the subscription expired** — renew it with your provider;
- **traffic is used up** — wait for the package to reset, or renew;
- **the device is not identified** — the provider requires device identification;
- **the device limit is reached** — free a slot in your account.

After renewing, refresh the subscription with the icon in the home screen header.

## Latency is red, or there is a cross

Red means the node answers slower than 400 ms, a cross means it did not answer at all.
Tap the bolt on the Servers tab to re-measure and pick another node. Right after switching
between Wi-Fi and mobile data the first measurements can be inflated — that is normal,
try again in a few seconds.

## The internet is gone completely

Turn the tunnel off with the button on the home screen and check whether the network works
without it. If it does, switch servers and reconnect; if the problem persists, attach the logs
(**More → Logs → Export**) to a support request.

## Some apps must bypass the tunnel

**More → Apps** — choose "Deny selected apps" and tick them. Banking apps and local services
are the usual candidates.

## The app asks for notification permission

From Android 13 on, without it you cannot see the ongoing tunnel notification, and the system
is quicker to put the service to sleep. It is worth granting.

## How do I change the language or the theme

**More → Appearance and behaviour**: "Dark mode" (follow system, always light, always dark)
and "Language" (follow system, English, Russian).

## The widget and the quick-settings tile

The 1×1 widget is added like any widget from the home screen, the tile from Android's
quick-settings editor. Both toggle the tunnel without opening the app.

## Automation: Tasker and friends

The client understands the external commands "start", "stop" and "toggle". They are
forbidden by default — otherwise any installed app could switch the VPN off without asking.
One switch allows them: **More → Application → "Allow external control"**. The app's own
shortcuts, the tile and the widget do not depend on this setting and always work.
