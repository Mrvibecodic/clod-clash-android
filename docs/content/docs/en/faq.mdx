## The connection drops while the phone is in my pocket

Android puts background apps to sleep. In **More → Connection** turn on:

1. **Unrestricted battery** — lift the restriction for the app.
2. **Always-on connection** — turn on always-on VPN in the system.

If the first request after sleep hangs, **More → Network → Keep the tunnel active while the
screen is off** helps — at the cost of noticeable battery drain.

Xiaomi, Huawei, Samsung and Oppo phones also have their own autostart and "memory protection"
lists — the app has to be added there by hand.

## The server list is empty

Most often it is the subscription. The app names the reason:

- **Subscription expired** — renew it with your provider;
- **Out of traffic** — wait for the refill or renew;
- **Device not identified** — turn on **More → Look and behavior → Identify this device**;
- **Device limit reached** — contact your provider's support;
- **The provider sent no servers** — a question for the provider.

After renewing, update the subscription with the icon in the home screen header.

## Latency is red or shows a dash

Red means the server answers slower than 400 ms (or the provider's bound), a red dash means it did
not answer. Tap the lightning bolt on the Servers tab and pick another server. Right after switching
between Wi-Fi and mobile data the first measurements can be inflated — try again in a few seconds.

In a group that picks its server itself (auto select, fallback), a server you pick by hand gets
pinned — it says "Pinned" under it. Tap it again to let the group pick by itself.

## No internet at all

Turn the tunnel off and check whether the network works without it. If it does, switch servers
and connect again. If that does not help, collect a log: **More → Logs → Core log** (start
recording), reproduce the problem, **Stop recording**, open the log and tap **Export**. Attach
the file to your support request.

## Some apps should bypass the tunnel

**More → Apps** → "Deny selected apps" and tick them. People usually do this with banking apps
and local services.

## How do I switch language or theme

**More → Look and behavior**: "Dark mode" (system default, always light, always dark) and
"Language" (system default, English, Russian).

## Widget, tile and shortcuts

The 1×1 widget is added from the home screen, the tile from the quick settings editor, and the
"Toggle", "Connect", "Disconnect" shortcuts with a long press on the app icon. All of them switch
the tunnel without opening the app.

## Automation: Tasker and the like

The client understands the external commands "start", "stop" and "toggle". They are blocked by
default so that another app cannot turn your VPN off without asking. To allow them —
**More → Look and behavior → Allow external control**.
